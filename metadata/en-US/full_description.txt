Iris is a privacy-centric, feature-rich keyboard for Android, built on the lightweight foundation of Simple Keyboard. Iris extends typing into a powerful productivity workspace by integrating locally-run AI models, an offline translation suite, mechanical switch sound synthesis, and advanced clipboard tools.

<b>Key Features:</b>

* <b>AI Copilot Studio:</b> Interact with AI backends (Ollama for local networks, Gemini, or custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints) directly inside your keyboard toolbar. Make text adjustments (Smart Compose, Grammar Fix, Simplify) without switching apps.
* <b>Translation Suite:</b> Translate text instantly using offline Google ML Kit models (requires MlKit flavor), web scraping, or custom AI translations.
* <b>Synthesized Switch Soundpacks:</b> Low-latency procedural audio engine that generates satisfying typing clacks, including bubble wrap, retro typewriter, Cherry MX switch clicks, or customizable sampled sound packs from remote archives.
* <b>Advanced Clipboard Manager:</b> Keeps an organized history of your copied texts with a quick paste chip bar.
* <b>Media & Expression:</b> Complete GIF search engine (Tenor, Giphy, or Klipy) and customizable emoji quick bar.
* <b>Personalization:</b> Choose geometric key cap shapes (Circle, Squircle, Hexagon, Square), adjust key size scales, change horizontal/vertical key gaps, and set custom key or keyboard-wide background images.
* <b>Built-in Themes & AMOLED Black:</b> High contrast themes with key color customizations and battery-saving true black mode.
* <b>FOSS & Privacy-First:</b> No trackers, analytics, or background telemetry. Supports Android MDM Managed Configurations for enterprise deployments.
